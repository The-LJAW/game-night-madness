Game Night Madness

Preview. This copy uses a sample shelf and runs on one phone. The live version loads your BoardGameGeek shelf, and everyone votes from their own phone.

Can't decide what to play?

Let the bracket decide

Pull the games from your BoardGameGeek shelf, seed them into a tournament-style bracket, and let everyone at the table vote round by round until one game is left standing.
